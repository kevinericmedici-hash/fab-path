A FAB BUYS, IT DOESN'T BUILD

No single company builds everything a chip needs

a fab assembles tools and materials it didn't invent

A fab doesn't manufacture its own scanners, etchers, or silicon wafers. It buys them from a small set of specialized suppliers, each of whom focuses on one piece of the puzzle and sells it to every fab in the world that needs it.

A modern chip depends on an entire ecosystem the fab itself never touches directly, until it arrives.

THE SUPPLY CHAIN

Three streams, all feeding one fab

Equipment makers supply the scanners, etchers, and deposition tools. Materials suppliers provide wafers, gases, chemicals, and photoresist. EDA software designs and verifies the chip before any of it is built. All three converge on the fab itself.

The fab is where these three separate industries meet and become one product.
