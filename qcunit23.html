LITHOGRAPHY

Drawing the circuit with light

Resist A light-sensitive polymer layer.
Expose and develop Optical for most of the chip, electron beam for junctions.
Etch and strip Cut the metal, then clean thoroughly.
The same steps that make computer chips make qubits.

ETCHING

Wet or dry?

Wet Etches in every direction and undercuts the mask.
Dry A directional plasma makes vertical walls.
Over-etch A shallow trench can lower surface loss.
Dry etching is the standard for fine qubit features.

LIFTOFF

Patterning without etching

Resist first Patterned with an overhang.
Evaporate Metal arrives in straight lines.
Dissolve The unwanted metal lifts away.
Josephson junctions are always made by liftoff.

IMPEDANCE

Design a 50-ohm line

50 Ω The impedance of every cable in the fridge.
A ratio Strip width to gap sets the impedance.
Precision Widths must hold to a fraction of a micrometre.
Mismatched lines reflect signals.

DEFECTS

Fab detective

Shorts and opens Break circuits.
Residues Add loss.
Size errors Shift frequencies.
Each fab mistake leaves its own fingerprint in the measurements.
