WAFER-LEVEL PROCESS

Doping

Doping deliberately changes the electrical properties of silicon by introducing selected impurity atoms.

Diffusion

Listed as one semiconductor fabrication method for introducing dopants.

Ion Implantation

Another semiconductor processing method used to introduce dopants.

Doping allows engineers to create regions with different electrical behavior within the same silicon wafer.

THIN-FILM DEPOSITION

Add material where you need it.

Thin-film deposition creates material layers that can later be patterned into functional structures.

CVD / LPCVD

Chemical vapor deposition can form thin films such as polysilicon and other process layers.

Evaporation

A deposition technique commonly used for thin material layers.

Sputtering

Another thin-film deposition method used in microfabrication.

Microfabrication alternates between adding material and removing material.
