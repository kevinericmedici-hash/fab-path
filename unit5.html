GOWNING

The operator is a contamination source.

Cleanroom clothing helps isolate the wafer from particles produced by the human body.

🥼

Cleanroom Suit

Covers normal clothing and helps contain fibers and particles.

🧤

Gloves

Help prevent oils, salts, and particles from transferring from hands to wafers.

😷

Face & Hair Covering

Reduces contamination from hair, skin, and respiratory droplets.

🥽

Eye Protection

Provides protection while working around chemicals and fabrication equipment.

Cleanroom rule: Protect the wafer from yourself.

CLEANROOM BEHAVIOR

How you move matters.

Fast movement can disturb particles and increase contamination inside the work environment.

✓ Move Carefully

Avoid unnecessary motion around exposed wafers.

✓ Handle Correctly

Use appropriate wafer-handling tools when possible.

✕ Avoid Bare-Hand Contact

Fingerprints contain oils, salts, and particles.

✕ Avoid Unnecessary Items

Personal items can introduce additional particles and contamination.

Good cleanroom behavior is part of the fabrication process itself.
