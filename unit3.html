MEMS SENSOR

Accelerometers

📱

An accelerometer measures acceleration or motion.

A tiny suspended mass can move when acceleration occurs. That displacement can then be converted into an electrical signal.

Accelerometers are used in phones, vehicles, wearables, navigation systems, and more.

MEMS SENSOR

Gyroscopes

🔄

MEMS gyroscopes detect rotation or angular motion.

They are often combined with accelerometers to help determine motion and orientation.

Common applications include smartphones, drones, vehicles, controllers, and navigation.

APPLICATIONS

MEMS are everywhere.

🚗 Inertial

Accelerometers and gyroscopes

🧬 BioMEMS

Lab-on-chip and microfluidics

🔬 Optical

Micromirrors and optical systems

📡 RF MEMS

Switches, filters, and resonators

🌡️ Sensors

Pressure and environmental sensing

📱 Consumer

Phones, wearables, and electronics
