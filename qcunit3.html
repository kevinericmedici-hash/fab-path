RECAP: CLASSICAL BITS

How a bit is stored in hardware

Always definite A bit is either 0 or 1, stored as charge, voltage or magnetism.
Reading is harmless Looking at a bit doesn't change it.
n bits Hold one of 2n patterns at a time.
Classical information is definite: you can always read it, copy it and check it.

QUBITS

A qubit holds a blend of 0 and 1

The state |ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩, where α and β are amplitudes.
The odds Reading gives 0 with probability |α|2 and 1 with probability |β|2.
The catch Afterwards the qubit is left in whatever you read.
A qubit isn't 'secretly 0 or 1': its blend is real, and it disappears when you look.

PICTURE IT

Every qubit state is an arrow on a sphere

North pole |0⟩.
South pole |1⟩.
Everywhere else A blend. The height sets the odds; the direction around is the phase.
This picture is called the Bloch sphere. The next unit explores it in detail.

MORE QUBITS

n qubits, 2ⁿ amplitudes

Three qubits Eight amplitudes at once: |000⟩ to |111⟩.
Ten qubits 1,024 amplitudes.
But measuring Gives back only n plain bits.
The power is in the amplitudes; the challenge is getting a useful answer out of them.

CHECK YOURSELF

Bit or qubit?

Bits Definite, readable, copyable.
Qubits Blends that collapse when read, and can't be copied.
Both Give a 0 or a 1 when you read them, and can be built on chips.
