THE BIG PICTURE

From wafer to wired chip

Front end of line Builds the transistors themselves, in the top few hundred nanometers of silicon
Middle of line Adds the contacts that connect transistors to the first wiring layer
Back end of line Stacks a dozen or more metal wiring levels above
Scale Well over a thousand process steps, taking weeks to months for a single wafer
A transistor is the result of a long, ordered recipe, and each step prepares the next.

ALIGNMENT

Why the gate is self-aligned

If the source and drain were patterned with their own mask, any misalignment would leave one side overlapping the gate too much and the other side with a gap. The fix: build the gate first, then use the gate itself as the mask for the implant. The source and drain edges can then only land where the gate is, however far the lithography wanders.

Self-alignment is what makes billion-transistor chips possible.

THE TOOLS

Four kinds of machine

Lithography EUV light at 13.5 nm and multiple patterning print features far below the wavelength
Deposition and epitaxy Atomic layer deposition lays high-k films one atomic layer at a time; epitaxy grows SiGe and doped source/drain
Etch and polish Plasma etching cuts the patterns, and chemical-mechanical polishing flattens each level
Metrology Optical and electron-beam tools check dimensions of a few nanometers on every wafer
Every step is a tool you can also meet in the MEMS and Sand-to-Chip courses.
