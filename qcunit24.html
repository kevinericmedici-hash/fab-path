SHADOW EVAPORATION

Make a junction with a shadow

The bridge Suspended resist casts a shadow.
Two angles Evaporate, oxidise, evaporate again.
One pump-down The barrier never sees air.
A clever geometry makes a nanometre barrier without patterning it.

GEOMETRY

Aim the overlap

Shift h · tan θ for each evaporation.
Overlap 2h · tan θ minus the bridge width.
Target About 0.2 µm.
Resist thickness and angle matter as much as the mask.

OXIDATION

Grow the tunnel barrier

Exposure Oxygen pressure × time.
Self-limiting Thickness grows only slowly with exposure.
Resistance Rn = (resistance × area) ÷ area.
Two knobs, area and oxidation, set every junction.

ALTERNATIVE

The Manhattan junction

Two crossing lines No suspended bridge needed.
Rotate 90° Each evaporation fills one line.
Bandage A final layer connects the junction to the circuit.
Simpler resist, more reproducible junctions.

VARIATION

No two junctions are identical

Edge roughness A few nanometres changes the area.
Frequency A 2% spread in resistance gives about 1% in frequency.
State of the art About 1 to 2% spread in resistance.
Spread is why frequency targeting needs its own step.
