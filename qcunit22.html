DEPOSITION

Four ways to lay down metal

Sputtering An argon plasma knocks atoms off a target.
Evaporation Atoms fly in straight lines: needed for junctions.
Hot or reactive Heated wafers and nitrogen gas change the film.
The deposition method shapes a film's crystal and its loss.

THE METALS

Four superconducting films

Aluminium The junctions.
Niobium and tantalum Pads, resonators and wiring.
TiN Ultra-low-loss resonators.
A transmon is usually made of two metals.

OXIDES

The oxide on top

Native oxide Grows within minutes in air.
TLS defects Flipping atoms that absorb qubit energy.
Fixes Metals with benign oxides, or capping in vacuum.
A film's top few nanometres can matter more than the rest.

CRYSTAL PHASE
