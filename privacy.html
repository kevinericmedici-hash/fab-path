PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026. The short version: Fab Path collects as little as it can, never sells your data, and shows no ads.

Who we are

Fab Path (fab-path.com and the Fab Path mobile app) is run by Kevin Medici ("Fab Path", "we", "us"). Questions about this policy go to kevin.eric.medici@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • If you don't sign in: nothing is sent to us. Your progress, quiz scores, and settings are saved only in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • If you create an account: your email address, your password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we can never see it), and your saved progress (lessons completed, quiz and game results, and practice history) so it can follow you across devices.
  • If you send feedback: your message, its category, and, only if you type it in, your email address. If you're signed in, the feedback is linked to your account.
  • Technical data: like any website, our hosting and sign-in providers automatically log basic request data such as IP address, browser type, and time of access, for security and to keep the service running.

We don't collect your name, location, payment details, or anything from your contacts, camera, or microphone.

How we use it

  • To run your account: signing you in, sending one-time sign-in codes, and syncing your progress.
  • To read and act on feedback, and to reply if you left an email.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We don't sell your information, share it for advertising, or use it to build marketing profiles. We don't run ads or third-party trackers, and our fonts are hosted on our own site.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the site, and only what each one needs:

  • Supabase stores accounts, progress, and feedback, and sends sign-in emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to a buyer or successor if Fab Path is ever sold or reorganized, who would then be bound by this policy.

Cookies and local storage

Fab Path doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to save progress and, if you sign in, to keep you signed in. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

How long we keep it

Account data and progress are kept until you delete your account. Feedback is kept as long as it's useful for improving the site. Deleting your account permanently removes your saved progress; feedback you sent stays but is no longer linked to your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Use it without an account. Every lesson, quiz, and game works signed out.
  • Delete your account yourself. Sign in, open the account menu, and choose Delete account. Your account and saved progress are removed immediately.
  • Access or correct your data. Email kevin.eric.medici@gmail.com from the address on your account and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, such as receiving a copy of your data or objecting to how it's used. Contact us and we'll honor them.

Children

Fab Path is built for high school students, college students, and adults. Accounts are not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules make sure each account can only read and change its own progress. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects how we use data you've already given us, we'll tell signed-in users before it takes effect.
