SOURCES

Sources and further reading

Fab Path's lessons, questions, and illustrations are written and drawn in our own words. These are the standard references behind the ideas, and the best places to go deeper.

Sand to Chip

  • J. D. Plummer, M. D. Deal, and P. B. Griffin, Silicon VLSI Technology: Fundamentals, Practice, and Modeling. Prentice Hall, 2000.
  • P. Van Zant, Microchip Fabrication: A Practical Guide to Semiconductor Processing, 6th ed. McGraw-Hill, 2014.
  • R. C. Jaeger, Introduction to Microelectronic Fabrication, 2nd ed. Prentice Hall, 2002.
  • B. E. Deal and A. S. Grove, "General relationship for the thermal oxidation of silicon," Journal of Applied Physics, vol. 36, pp. 3770–3778, 1965.
  • G. E. Moore, "Cramming more components onto integrated circuits," Electronics, vol. 38, no. 8, 1965.
  • IEEE, International Roadmap for Devices and Systems (IRDS), irds.ieee.org.

MEMS & Microfabrication

  • S. A. Campbell, Fabrication Engineering at the Micro- and Nanoscale, 4th ed. Oxford University Press.
  • S. Franssila, Introduction to Microfabrication, 2nd ed. Wiley, 2010.
  • M. J. Madou, Fundamentals of Microfabrication and Nanotechnology, 3rd ed. CRC Press, 2011.
  • S. D. Senturia, Microsystem Design. Kluwer Academic Publishers, 2001.

Supercapacitors

  • K. Medici, New Generation of High-Power Density 3D Interdigitated Supercapacitors, M.S. thesis, San Diego State University, 2026. Primary source for this course
  • B. E. Conway, Electrochemical Supercapacitors: Scientific Fundamentals and Technological Applications. Kluwer Academic / Plenum, 1999.
  • P. Simon and Y. Gogotsi, "Materials for electrochemical capacitors," Nature Materials, vol. 7, pp. 845–854, 2008.

BioFETs & MOSFETs

  • S. M. Sze and K. K. Ng, Physics of Semiconductor Devices, 3rd ed. Wiley, 2007.
  • R. F. Pierret, Semiconductor Device Fundamentals. Addison-Wesley, 1996.
  • B. G. Streetman and S. K. Banerjee, Solid State Electronic Devices, 7th ed. Pearson, 2015.
  • P. Bergveld, "Development of an ion-sensitive solid-state device for neurophysiological measurements," IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, vol. BME-17, no. 1, pp. 70–71, 1970.
  • M. J. Schöning and A. Poghossian, "Recent advances in biologically sensitive field-effect transistors (BioFETs)," Analyst, vol. 127, pp. 1137–1151, 2002.

Accuracy

Numbers in the lessons are typical textbook or published values, usually rounded, and real processes vary by fab and tool. Treat any figure as a starting point and check it against a primary source before relying on it for design or research.
