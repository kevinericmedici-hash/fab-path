MEMS & Microfabrication

Much of this course is built on Dr. Samuel Kassegne's graduate MEMS course at San Diego State University, and on the references his lectures draw from.

  • S. Kassegne, ME 685: MEMS Design and Application, lecture series, Department of Mechanical Engineering, San Diego State University, 2024–2025. Lectures: Introduction; Review of Microfabrication Technology; PolyMUMPs: MEMS Fabrication Technology; MEMS Accelerometer Design; Optical MEMS Design; Microfluidics Device Design; MEMS Packaging. Primary source for this course

Accuracy

Numbers in the lessons are typical textbook or published values, usually rounded, and real processes vary by fab and tool. Treat any figure as a starting point and check it against a primary source before relying on it for design or research.
