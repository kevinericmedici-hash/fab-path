UNIT 1 • MEMS FOUNDATIONS

What are MEMS?

MEMS stands for Microelectromechanical Systems.

⚙️

MEMS combine tiny mechanical structures with electrical components to sense, move, control, or interact with the physical world.

Key idea MEMS bring mechanical and electrical behavior together at microscopic scales.

SENSING & ACTUATION

Sensors vs. Actuators

👁️

Sensor

Detects a physical quantity and converts it into a useful signal.

⚙️

Actuator

Uses energy or a control signal to create physical movement or force.

A MEMS device may sense, actuate, or do both as part of a larger system.

SCALE

How small is the microscale?

1 millimeter 10⁻³ m
1 micrometer 10⁻⁶ m
1 nanometer 10⁻⁹ m
Remember: 1 mm = 1,000 µm

1 µm = 1,000 nm

SCALING LAWS

Shrinking changes physics.

If every dimension shrinks by a factor of 10:

Length

L

1 / 10

Area

L²

1 / 100

Volume

L³

1 / 1,000
Area and volume do not shrink at the same rate.

SURFACE EFFECTS

Surface area matters more.

◻️ → ▫️ → ·

As an object becomes smaller, its volume decreases faster than its surface area.

That means the surface-area-to-volume ratio increases as size decreases.

Smaller devices are influenced more strongly by surface-related effects.

MICROSCALE PHYSICS

Different forces dominate.

Gravity

Becomes relatively less significant as structures shrink.

Electrostatics

Can remain very useful for sensing and actuation.

MEMS cannot simply be designed as tiny versions of ordinary machines.

MEMS SENSOR

Accelerometers

📱

An accelerometer measures acceleration or motion.

A tiny suspended mass can move when acceleration occurs. That displacement can then be converted into an electrical signal.

Accelerometers are used in phones, vehicles, wearables, navigation systems, and more.

MEMS SENSOR

Gyroscopes

🔄

MEMS gyroscopes detect rotation or angular motion.

They are often combined with accelerometers to help determine motion and orientation.

Common applications include smartphones, drones, vehicles, controllers, and navigation.

APPLICATIONS

MEMS are everywhere.

🚗 Inertial

Accelerometers and gyroscopes

🧬 BioMEMS

Lab-on-chip and microfluidics

🔬 Optical

Micromirrors and optical systems

📡 RF MEMS

Switches, filters, and resonators

🌡️ Sensors

Pressure and environmental sensing

📱 Consumer

Phones, wearables, and electronics
