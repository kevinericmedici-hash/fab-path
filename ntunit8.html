MADE FROM

From tryptophan

Start The amino acid tryptophan, from the diet
Where in the body Most of the body's serotonin is made in the gut, and only a small share is in the brain
In the brain Neurons in the raphe nuclei of the brainstem send fibers across the brain
Serotonin is a gut molecule as much as a brain molecule.

SSRIs

Block the cleanup

Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors block the serotonin transporter, so serotonin lingers in the cleft.

A drug that blocks the off switch lengthens the message.

REALITY CHECK

Not instant, not simple

Timing SSRIs raise serotonin within hours, but benefits for mood often take weeks
Meaning That delay shows the effect involves slower adaptation, not just higher levels
Receptors Serotonin acts on more than a dozen receptor subtypes, so drugs differ in effects
The picture is more complicated than a simple chemical imbalance.

MEASURING IT

A tough one to catch

Serotonin is present at very low levels and tends to stick to electrodes and foul them, which makes it one of the harder neurotransmitters to measure in real time. Engineers use special coatings and waveforms to cope.

A sensor has to be selective and also resist sticking.
