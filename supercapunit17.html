SHAPE-SHIFTING • STRETCHABLE

Bend, stretch, fold

🦋
Serpentine bridges Wavy, horseshoe-shaped connections unfold when stretched, so the stiff conductor barely strains
Buckled films Electrodes bonded to a pre-stretched elastomer wrinkle when released, then flatten on stretch
Origami / kirigami Fold and cut patterns let a sheet expand, bend, or collapse compactly

SHAPE-SHIFTING • MADE DIFFERENTLY

Printed, see-through, and lattice

Printed

Screen or inkjet printing of electrodes onto paper or plastic, even roll to roll

Transparent

Ultrathin films or sparse meshes let light through, at the cost of less stored charge

3D-printed lattices

Designed open channels give ions fast routes through thick electrodes

The manufacturing method becomes part of the geometry: what you can print decides what you can shape.

SHAPE-SHIFTING • MULTIFUNCTIONAL

When the battery is the body

✈️

Structural

A carbon-fiber composite that carries mechanical load and stores energy, so a panel can double as a power source.

🫀

Implantable

Soft, thin devices that conform to tissue; researchers also explore biodegradable ones that dissolve when their job is done.

Here the shape is set by the host: the aircraft skin or the organ surface.
