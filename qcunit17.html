THE KEY COMPONENT

A sandwich of aluminium and oxide

Two electrodes Aluminium strips that cross.
The barrier 1 to 2 nm of aluminium oxide between them.
Tiny The overlap is about 0.2 × 0.2 µm.
Every superconducting qubit is built around this one structure.

TUNNELLING

Crossing an insulator

Quantum leakage The pair wave leaks through the thin barrier.
Exponential 0.1 nm more oxide cuts the current by about e (2.7 times).
Sensitive Atom-scale control decides the qubit's frequency.
The barrier must be uniform almost atom by atom.

JOSEPHSON EFFECTS

Current with no voltage

DC effect Below Ic, current flows with zero voltage.
AC effect Above it, a voltage V makes it oscillate at 2eV/h.
1962 Brian Josephson predicted both (Nobel Prize, 1973).
Qubits always run below the critical current.

NONLINEARITY

An inductor with a twist

Phase The current sets the phase: I = Ic sin φ.
Inductance LJ grows as the current grows.
The key Nonlinear and lossless: exactly what a qubit needs.
The junction is the only circuit element that is both nonlinear and lossless.

THE SQUID

Two junctions make a tunable one

SQUID A small loop containing two junctions.
Flux tunes it EJ falls as |cos(πΦ/Φ0)|.
Sweet spot At the top of the curve, flux noise matters least.
Tunable qubits and couplers are built from SQUIDs.
