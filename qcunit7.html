WAVES

Waves can add or cancel

In step They add up: constructive interference.
Opposite They cancel: destructive interference.
Brightness Goes as amplitude squared.
Quantum amplitudes interfere just like waves.

ONE QUBIT

Interference with a single photon

Two paths The photon's paths are its |0⟩ and |1⟩.
Beam splitters Act like Hadamard gates.
The phase Decides which detector fires: cos2(φ/2).
Every photon takes both paths, and the paths interfere.

THE TRICK

The recipe behind quantum algorithms

Spread Put every possible answer in superposition.
Mark Flip the sign of the right answer's amplitude.
Interfere Make wrong answers cancel and the right one grow, then measure.
It isn't 'trying every answer at once': it's choreographing the cancellations.

GROVER'S SEARCH

Finding a needle with interference

Classical About N/2 tries to find one item in N.
Quantum About (π/4)√N Grover steps.
Timing Too many steps and the odds fall again.
A quadratic speedup: useful, but not magic.

SPEEDUPS

How much faster depends on the problem

Searching Quadratic speedup.
Factoring and chemistry Exponential speedups for some problems.
Everyday tasks No speedup at all.
Quantum computers are specialists, not faster versions of your laptop.
