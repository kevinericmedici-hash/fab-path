Bigger particle → bigger acoustic force, same viscous drag

The standing-wave force scales with particle volume, but Stokes drag only scales with radius — so past a certain drive strength, larger cells cross the channel to the node well before smaller ones do. The real chip this is modeled on drives its IDTs at ~10V, launching a wave at a wavelength of about 240 µm — a resonance the design team confirmed by simulation at 16.473 MHz before ever cutting a wafer.
