WHY BRIDGES

Tying the ground together

Split grounds Lines cut the ground plane into pieces.
Unwanted modes Slotline modes and floating islands steal energy.
Airbridges Small arches tie the ground pieces together.
Bridges keep the ground plane one piece.

FABRICATION

Building an airbridge

Scaffold A resist block, reflowed into a hump.
Metal Aluminium deposited over it and patterned.
Release Dissolving the resist leaves a free arch.
A temporary scaffold makes a permanent bridge.

CROSSTALK

Keep signals in their own lines

Leakage A pulse for one qubit nudges another.
Distance Helps, but chip area is precious.
Shielding A grounded strip stitched with bridges.
Good layout keeps every pulse where it belongs.

MECHANICS

How long can a bridge be?

Sag Grows steeply with span.
Thickness Thicker metal is stiffer.
Collapse A sagging bridge can short the line below.
Even a quantum chip has to obey mechanics.

PLACEMENT

Bridge or no bridge?

Stitching Along long lines that split the ground.
Crossovers Where one line must cross another.
Islands Wherever a ground patch would float.
Bridges are small, but a large chip needs thousands.
