ω₀ = √(k/m)  ·  Q = mω₀/b  ·  S = m/k  ·  TNEA = √(4kBTb)/m

The same mass and spring set both the frequency and the sensitivity — that's why a stiffer spring always trades sensitivity for speed. The air gap sets the damping (b) through Couette flow, which is what actually controls the quality factor and the noise floor.
