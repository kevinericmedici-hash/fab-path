F = ε₀AV² / 2g²

Force scales with the square of voltage but falls off with the square of the gap — which is why closing the gap works so much faster than raising the voltage. This exact relationship is what drives an accelerometer's electrostatic self-test structures, and what makes pull-in happen so suddenly in a torsion mirror.
