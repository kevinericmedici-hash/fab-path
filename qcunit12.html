GATES ARE ROTATIONS

Turning the Bloch sphere

X, Y, Z Half turns about the three axes.
H Swaps |0⟩ and |+⟩.
S and T Quarter and eighth turns: they change only the phase.
Every single-qubit gate is just a rotation.

PUZZLE

Steer the qubit

Goal Rotate the arrow onto the target in as few gates as possible.
Why it matters Fewer gates mean fewer chances for errors.
Compilers Real software does this optimisation automatically.
Programming a quantum computer means choosing rotations.

TWO QUBITS

Two-qubit gates

CNOT Flips B when A is 1.
CZ and SWAP A sign flip on |11⟩, and an exchange.
Universal One entangling gate plus rotations can build any program.
Two-qubit gates are the slowest and least accurate, and the most important.

FIDELITY

Why 99.9% isn't good enough

Fidelity How close a gate comes to perfect.
It compounds Success ≈ FN for N gates.
At 99.9% A thousand gates leaves about a 37% chance of no error.
No physical qubit is good enough alone: hence error correction.

PULSES

How gates are really done

Microwave pulses Tens of nanoseconds long.
Leakage Sharp pulses can kick the qubit up to |2⟩.
Shaping Smooth pulses (and designs like DRAG) keep it in its two levels.
Precise control electronics are as important as the qubits themselves.
