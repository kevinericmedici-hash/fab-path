ENERGY LEVELS

Quantum things have energy levels

Only certain energies Atoms and circuits have fixed levels, like rungs on a ladder.
E = hf A photon is absorbed only if its energy matches a gap.
Selectivity You can address one transition and leave the others alone.
Quantised levels are what make precise qubit control possible.

PICK TWO LEVELS

Why a qubit needs uneven steps

Even ladder A plain circuit's levels are evenly spaced, so one pulse drives them all.
Uneven ladder A transmon's steps shrink a little each time.
The qubit Drive exactly the 0→1 frequency and only those two levels respond.
The Josephson junction makes the ladder uneven; you'll build one in Part 3.

MICROWAVES

Qubits live in the microwave band

Superconducting qubits About 4–8 GHz, near Wi-Fi.
Spin qubits About 10–40 GHz.
Ions and atoms Lasers, plus microwave clock transitions.
Quantum computing borrows heavily from RF and microwave engineering.

COLDER THAN SPACE

Why everything must be so cold

A 5 GHz qubit Its energy matches heat at only about 0.24 kelvin.
Room temperature Heat would flip it about half the time.
10 millikelvin The chance drops to about one in a hundred billion.
Dilution refrigerators make superconducting qubits possible.

DRIVING A QUBIT

Rabi oscillations

A resonant drive Rotates the qubit back and forth between 0 and 1.
A π pulse Half a cycle: flips 0 into 1, the quantum NOT gate.
Detuning Drive at the wrong frequency and it never fully flips.
Every gate on a superconducting chip is a carefully timed microwave pulse.
