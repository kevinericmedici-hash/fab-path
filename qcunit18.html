THE OSCILLATOR

A circuit that rings

LC circuit Energy swaps between the capacitor and the inductor.
f = 1/(2π√(LC)) Nanohenries and femtofarads give gigahertz.
Equal steps Its quantum energy levels are evenly spaced.
An LC circuit is a quantum oscillator, but not yet a qubit.

ANHARMONICITY

Make the steps uneven

The problem With equal steps, a pulse climbs the whole ladder.
The junction Swap the coil for a junction and the upper steps shrink.
Transmon An anharmonicity of about −200 to −300 MHz.
Uneven steps let us use just the bottom two levels.

CHARGE NOISE

From Cooper-pair box to transmon

Cooper-pair box 1999: the first superconducting qubit, very charge sensitive.
Big capacitor Raises EJ/EC to about 50.
Transmon 2007: charge noise all but disappears.
One design change made superconducting qubits practical.

DESIGN

Design a 5 GHz transmon

EC Set by the size of the capacitor pads.
EJ Set by the junction.
The formula f01 ≈ √(8EJEC) − EC.
Fabrication's job is to build exactly these two numbers.

VARIATIONS

The transmon family

Pads and crosses Different shapes, same physics.
Tunable A SQUID lets the frequency move.
3D A transmon in a metal box lives longer, but scales poorly.
Most of today's superconducting processors use transmons.
