ATOMS AND IONS

Nature's identical qubits

Trapped ions Record gate accuracy, but slow gates and complex lasers.
Neutral atoms Hundreds of atoms in laser tweezers, rearranged on demand.
The chip side Trap chips, photonics and optics are all fabricated.
Perfectly identical qubits, held by microfabricated devices.

Fabrication Spins and photonics reuse chip factories most directly.
Scale Atoms scale quickly today; everyone faces wiring and control limits.
