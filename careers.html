CAREER PATHS

Chip and device careers, mapped to what you can learn.

Microfabrication skills open doors across semiconductors, sensors, energy, medical devices and biotech. Pick a role to see what it involves and which Fab Path units and virtual labs build the foundations for it.

Where these roles exist

Foundries, integrated device makers, equipment suppliers, fabless design companies, packaging and test houses, medical device and biotech companies, national labs, and university cleanrooms all hire for these functions.

  • Foundries (e.g. TSMC, GlobalFoundries)
  • Device makers (e.g. Intel, Texas Instruments)
  • Equipment (e.g. ASML, Applied Materials, Lam Research)
  • Fabless design (e.g. Qualcomm, NVIDIA)
  • Packaging & test houses
  • Medical device & wearable sensor makers
  • Biotech & pharma R&D (organ-on-chip, drug testing)
  • Neurotechnology & brain-interface startups
  • Battery & energy-storage companies
  • National labs & university cleanrooms

An honest scope note. Fab Path teaches the fundamentals underneath these roles. It is not company-specific training, it doesn't cover any company's proprietary processes, and advanced-node topics such as EUV lithography are covered at an introductory level only. The virtual labs use teaching models, not production simulators.
